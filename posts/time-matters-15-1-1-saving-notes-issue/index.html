Time Matters 15.1.1 Saving Notes Issue

Date: Jun. 12, 2016

There is a minor bug in Time Matters 15.1.1. Whenever you Close a Note in Time Matters, whether you have made any changes or have made no changes at all, you will get a pop-up window saying:

Do you want to save changes?

All you have to do is click Yes or click No or press the spacebar to choose Yes.

I trust this annoyance will be fixed in the next release.
