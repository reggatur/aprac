Time Matters Power View Fix

Date: Oct. 8, 2016    Categories: lists , troubleshooting , upgrading

In Time Matters 15, the Power Views of Notes and TM Messages display HTML code, cluttering and wasting screen space.

Each time I perform a Time Matters upgrade for a law firm, I am thankful to Adam Bass for the fix he created. I published the Time Matters Power View fix earlier. Here is a tweak to that fix.

Before opening a Time Matters Power View HTML file to fix it:

  1. Right-click on the Power View HTML file.
  2. Click: Properties
  3. Uncheck the box: Read-only

That step is needed because current versions of Time Matters protect the Power View files by making them Read-only.
