Hotfix for Time Matters 16.2

Date: Jul. 21, 2017    Categories: Billing Matters , links , upgrading , updates

As noted on our Updates to Time Matters page we have not recommended upgrading to Time Matters 16.2 due to a number of issues listed there.

LexisNexis is now in the process of fixing two important issues affecting the Billing Matters BillFlow Manager and the link to QuickBooks, LexisNexis has pulled the Time Matters 16.2 release. They will announce the release of a Hotfix in the near future.

In the meantime, we do recommend upgrading to Time Matters 16.1 for most firms. Existing issues are covered in depth in our Time Matters Upgrade Guide and updated on our Updates to Time Matters page.
