WatchDox Secure Document Exchange Ended

Date: Mar. 28, 2016    Categories: document management , utilities

Due to performance problems and service outages, on March 31, 2016 LexisNexis ends the WatchDox service integrated in Time Matters 13, 14 and 15.

Our affiliated company, Active Online Inc., provides combined cloud backup and secure document sharing services that work well with Time Matters. For more information, please see:
https://securemyfirm.com

WatchDox is a secure document exchange service offered by BlackBerry. It was integrated in Time Matters 13, making it relatively easy to securely share documents and files on the Documents tabs of Matters with clients and others.

LexisNexis is investigating alternative services but has no current timetable for adding a secure document exchange feature to Time Matters. For Frequently Asked Questions about the end of WatchDox services, see: http://lawpractice.lexisnexis.com/FAQ-TimeMatters-WatchDox
