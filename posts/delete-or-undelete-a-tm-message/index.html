Delete or Undelete a TM Message

Date: Feb. 14, 2023    Categories: administration , troubleshooting

Delete a TM Message

In the TM Messenger window when a list of messages is displayed, the Delete button deletes the highlighted message. To delete multiple TM Messages, check their boxes and click:
TM Messenger Window Menu | Process | Delete Records

Undelete a TM Message

Go to: Main Menu | File | Recycle Bin | (long wait)

Press: Ctrl-End to go to the bottom (or scroll there, but that's slow)

Find your Message by Date / Subject / Date & Time Deleted

Check its checkbox

Click: Undelete (upper left of window).
