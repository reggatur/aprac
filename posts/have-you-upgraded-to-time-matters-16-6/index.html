Have You Upgraded to Time Matters 16.6?

Date: Jul. 28, 2021    Categories: updates , document management

It is easy to skip applying the latest point release of Time Matters and of other software for that matter.

I recommend that you upgrade to the latest Time Matters point release, 16.6, especially if you may have skipped some earlier Time Matters 16 point releases.

Time Matters Tip: To see what point release you have, go to:

Time Matters Main Menu | Help | About Time Matters

Press: Ctrl-F9. The release number will appear in the upper right corner of the window.

For firms that have used the Time Matters Documents features for many years, the number of Document records can grow large. The current release of Time Matters improves the speed of working with the Documents tab on your Matters.
