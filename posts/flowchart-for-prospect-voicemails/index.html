Flowchart for Prospect Voicemails

Date: Oct. 23, 2021    Categories: workflow , training , todos , organizing

Using a flowchart to diagram an internal process delivers these key advantages:

  • Clearly shows everyone involved what to do next at a glance

  • Prevents your eyes from glazing over looking at a wall of text

  • Promotes streamlining processes by visualizing opportunities to remove steps

  • Allows for quick modification and communication of your process

  • Assists in training and cross-training your people
