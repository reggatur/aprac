Time Matters Record Review

Date: Apr. 14, 2026    Categories: collaboration , related records

Time Matters has a Record Review function that enables people in your office to be alerted to the entry of a new record. This feature is useful for ensuring that everyone knows about that a new client has retained the firm.

After you are notified of a new client or event on the caledar, you can sign off on the new record using the Record Review function.

This post shows how to give yourself the right to uncheck the Review box on a record and end the Record Review for that record.

Launch the Time Matters Record Review panel

Review panel

In the Record Review panel, you may change Your Review Status using that drop-down field.

You may end the Record Review by checking the End box opposite your name.
