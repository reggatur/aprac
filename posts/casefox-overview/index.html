Each vendor participating in our review of cloud practice management applications submitted additional material about their service. Here is the material from CaseFox.

CaseFox

CaseFox is designed to help attorneys, virtual firms, psychiatrists, private investigators, consultants, accountants and other professionals with their timekeeping, invoicing, trust accounting, case management, calendaring and task management needs.

CaseFox can track both billable and non-billable hours and expenses, monitor un-billed hours, late payments and handle trust accounting, case documents and case notes. CaseFox even handles income expense accounting, conflict checks, discovery tracking/docketing, phone call logging, fee splitting rules and abbreviation-assisted time entries.
