MyCase

MyCase (mycase.com) web-based practice management software was founded by a lawyer for lawyers and was built to address the number one complaint across all State Bar Associations: insufficient attorney/client communication.

MyCase makes is easy for lawyers to stay organized and on top of their busy law offices, no matter what their areas of practice. Using MyCase, lawyers can effortlessly organize the critical practice challenges of their law firms– contacts, calendars, cases, documents, time tracking, and billing – in a way that is incredibly straightforward. In fact, MyCase is so simple that, unlike competing products, the learning curve is minimal and firms can simply sign up and begin working on cases in a matter of minutes.

Another significant benefit that MyCase offers busy lawyers is increased flexibility. Because MyCase is a cloud-based platform, it gives lawyers the freedom to practice law on their own terms. Using MyCase, lawyers can access case and client information wherever they may be, day or night. Taking their practices with them lets them take advantage of time spent commuting, waiting in court, or traveling. Attorneys aren't limited to a specific computer or device and will never again have worry about syncing information with their offices.

But what truly sets MyCase apart from the rest is its uniquely integrated client portal and communications features. The MyCase online portal makes it possible for lawyers to share calendars, documents and billing information with clients based on permissions they determine. Clients simply log on and view all recent activity to which they have been provided access--including new court dates, recently uploaded documents, and invoices--which they can then interact with in MyCase’s secure, encrypted online environment. So, for example, clients can upload or download documents, comment on documents or calendar events, and even pay invoices.

Successful legal professionals all over the world rely on MyCase every day to stay incredibly organized, easily communicate and collaborate with their clients while simultaneously managing and growing their practice. Because MyCase offers legal practice management in the cloud, lawyers can work from anywhere at any time, significantly increasing productivity. ?In other words, lawyers can practice more and manage less with MyCase.
