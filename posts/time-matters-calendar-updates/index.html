Time Matters Calendar Updates

Date: Jan. 16, 2017    Categories: calendar

In Time Matters 16, the Calendar has a number of important new options.

Back by popular demand is the Classic Calendar View: Detail View gives more ways to see your day, while bringing back the functionality of the Classic View in Time Matters 13 and earlier.

The Detail View is limited to one day at a time, but it does a better job of showing the Events of many staff on one screen.

There are still some issues in the Calendar. For more information, see: Calendar Quick Tips - Set 1 and Calendar Quick Tips - Set 2
