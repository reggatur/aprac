CosmoLex Overview

Date: Oct. 2, 2014    Categories: cloud , practice management

Authored by the vendor

When it comes to legal time tracking and billing, missed time and expense entries or hard-to-understand invoices translate to lost revenues, frustrated customers, poor collections and sagging profits. On top of that, law firms must manage trust accounts in compliance with state ethics requirements. A poorly set up billing system can make matters even worse but CosmoLex can help.

CosmoLex is cloud-based law practice management software that eliminates time tracking, billing and trust accounting issues. CosmoLex helps law firms manage matters, record advances, track time and expenses, generate bills, receive payments, reconcile bank statements and track client funds. With CosmoLex law practice becomes more efficient, productive and profitable.

Try the full version of CosmoLex free for 30 days with no obligation and no credit card required.

Log in to CosmoLex on any computer, mobile phone or tablet and you’ll take care of all your practice management chores — in one place:

  • Case Management
  • Calendar & Tasks (Google Calendar integration)
  • Time & Billing
  • Trust (IOLTA) Accounting
  • Fee Advances
  • Check Printing (quickbooks check format)
  • Payment & Collection
  • Bank Reconciliation
  • Contact Management
  • Docket Control / Conflict Check
